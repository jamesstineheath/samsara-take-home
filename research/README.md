# Research

Research is bounded. It exists to support decisions, not to become the submission.

Required buckets:

- Samsara product/current company context.
- Fleet maintenance daily workflow.
- Supervisor planning pain points.
- Data inputs likely available in Samsara.
- Metrics and operational vocabulary.

Current synthesized artifacts:

- `research/07-prompt-vs-current-product-opportunity-map.md` - compares the prompt with Samsara's current product page and identifies inferred opportunity areas.
- `research/08-personas-pain-points-and-competitive-intelligence.md` - persona, pain-point, review evidence, and competitor landscape synthesis.
- `research/09-competitor-deep-dives-primary-sources.md` - deeper Fleetio, MaintainX, and Fullbay analysis from primary competitor sources.
- `research/10-hertz-rental-fleet-day-in-life.md` - Hertz LAX rental-fleet operating model, day-in-life personas, canonical source list, and v1 product implications.
- `research/11-hertz-lax-systems-and-data-flow.md` - maps the Hertz LAX day-in-life to current systems, data objects, gaps, feasibility, and a visible Mermaid system/data flow diagram.
- `research/12-asset-uptime-metrics-and-strategy.md` - defines Asset Uptime metric hierarchy, product strategy, metric traps, and implications for open scope questions.

Stop rule: once the chosen v1 can be defended with sources and the remaining unknowns can be stated as assumptions, stop researching and build.
