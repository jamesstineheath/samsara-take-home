# Samsara Take-Home Workspace

This repo is for planning, building, and reviewing the Samsara Principal PM - Maintenance take-home.

Current boundary: planning and sparring only. Do not draft the memo or prototype solution until the strategy, scope, evidence plan, and decision gates are accepted.

## Working Files

- `docs/00-constraints-and-sources.md` - sourced assignment requirements, deadline, deliverables, and ambiguity.
- `docs/01-execution-strategy.md` - success definition, phases, decisions, workback schedule, and first-session agenda.
- `docs/02-decision-log.md` - human decisions and rejected alternatives.
- `docs/03-ai-provenance-log.md` - record of AI-generated vs. James-originated work.
- `sources/` - source inventory and source excerpts, not raw private emails unless intentionally added.
- `research/` - bounded domain and company research.
- `memo/` - memo drafts and final PDF/export later.
- `prototype/` - app code later.
- `review/` - live review notes, rehearsal script, and stress tests.

## Operating Rule

The winning submission should be specific, defensible, and easy to walk through live. If the work starts drifting into a broad CMMS clone, generic AI dashboard, or beautiful prototype without first-principles judgment, stop and return to the decision log.
